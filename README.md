# Velvet Hour — a heist in five rooms

A top-down stealth heist game, built for the [make.fun bounty "Make the best heist game"](https://make.fun/bounty/best-heist-game-20261122-0559).

Five rooms, one night. Read the cones, mind the sound, take the target and
walk out like the night itself. Every room has more than one way through:
shadow corridors, a vent-like shortcut behind a locked door, or straight
across the lit floor if your nerve holds.

- **Sound is a mechanic.** Walking is silent; running is loud and draws
  guards to the noise. Toss a decoy coin and they walk to the sound.
- **Light and shadow are the map.** Shadow halves a cone's reach and halves
  how fast the alarm rises. Smoke blinds every cone near you.
- **Tools at a cost.** Coins from loot buy smoke bombs, decoy coins and a
  lockpick — spend them on the planning screen before you go in.
- **The Plan.** Every heist opens with intel: a minimap with patrol routes,
  camera positions and the loot, so the getaway pays off the planning.
- **Five distinct rooms**, from the Gallery After Dark to the Crown Room,
  with sweeping guard patrols, rotating cameras and timing lasers.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static HTTP server and open `game/index.html`
in desktop Chrome. No network requests; all sound is synthesized in the browser.

WASD or arrows to move · SHIFT to run (loud) · 1 smoke · 2 decoy coin at
cursor · 3 lockpick (passive) · M mute · P pause.
